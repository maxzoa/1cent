## Interpretation

- `implemented` means a canonical catalog entry and equal REST, remote MCP and Buyer Bridge contract.
- `planned` is not advertised or sold and has a dated reason for deferral.
- `blocked_external` requires an external provider, credential, capability or cost proof.
- `unsafe` remains in the denominator and is intentionally not implemented.
- Machine-readable details, costs, latency, cacheability and risks are in the JSON and CSV files.
